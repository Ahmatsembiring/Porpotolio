# ISO 27001 Risk Register Template

Template sederhana untuk mencatat risiko keamanan informasi.

| Asset | Threat | Vulnerability | Impact | Likelihood | Risk Level | Control | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Web App | Unauthorized access | Weak authorization | High | Medium | High | Access control review | Mitigate |
| Database | Data leakage | Weak backup protection | High | Low | Medium | Encryption and backup policy | Mitigate |
| Cloud Storage | Public exposure | Misconfiguration | High | Medium | High | Storage policy and review | Mitigate |

## Kolom Penting

- Asset: sistem, data, atau proses yang dilindungi.
- Threat: potensi kejadian yang merugikan.
- Vulnerability: kelemahan yang dapat dieksploitasi.
- Impact: dampak bila risiko terjadi.
- Likelihood: kemungkinan terjadi.
- Control: kontrol keamanan yang diterapkan.
- Treatment: avoid, mitigate, transfer, atau accept.
